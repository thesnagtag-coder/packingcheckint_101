How this works

1. Create a trip and add each leg (place, country, dates) - climate auto-fills from the country.
2. Add each traveler and every bag they're carrying.
3. The dashboard builds your checklist and shows general climate + etiquette notes per leg.
4. Tick items as you pack and pick which bag each item goes in. Add or remove items any time - your master list is saved for next trip.
